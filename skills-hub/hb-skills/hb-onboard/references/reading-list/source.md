# Fictional reading-list observations

This is authored training data, not external research or a real delivery report.

## First observation

In a fictional local prototype, a reader saved three articles while online.
After closing the browser, disconnecting and reopening it, article titles were
available but the article bodies were not. This observation supports a distinction
between saving a reference and storing content; it does not establish how other
reading applications behave.

## Second observation

A later fictional prototype stored article bodies explicitly. After the same
restart and disconnection, two saved articles opened. The third linked to a page
that had required authentication and had not been stored. This does not prove
that the new prototype works for every article or that it was released.
