# Saving articles for offline use

Fictional exercise draft. Intended reader: someone designing a small reading list.

Saving an article reference does not establish that its body will be available
offline. In the first prototype observation, titles persisted but bodies did not.
[Training observations](source.md)

## Applicability and open questions

This lesson concerns the fictional prototype. Its behavior for authenticated
pages and other applications remains to be examined. Update this draft after
reviewing the second observation; do not infer delivery from the prototype tests.
